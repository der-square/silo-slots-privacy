# Privacy Policy — Silo Match

_Last updated: 2026-11-27_

**Who we are.** Silo Match (formerly Silo Slots) is operated by Square, BA2 1FJ, United Kingdom. Contact: bennetmarcus19@gmail.com. This is the controller of your data under the GDPR.

## What this game is
Silo Match is a match-3 puzzle and memory game for players aged 18 and over. Coins are a free in-game reward for playing. They cannot be bought, cashed out, transferred or exchanged for anything of value. The game contains no gambling of any kind.

## Age (18+)
When you first open the app we ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older. Your answer is stored only on your device. If you say you are under 18, the app will not let you play. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## No account, data stored on your device
You do not need an account and you do not give us your name, e-mail or phone number. Your game progress (coins, level, puzzle progress, settings, your age answer) is saved only on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted. We do not currently run a server that stores your progress.

## Advertising
The game is free and paid for by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to show ads, including optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for bonus coins or extra moves.

AdMob and its ad partners may collect and process device and usage data such as your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions, to deliver, measure and (if you agree) personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

### Your choices (GDPR / EEA, UK, Switzerland)
- **Consent:** In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can accept, decline or manage options.
- **Change your mind:** Open Settings → "Privacy & ad choices" in the app at any time to review or withdraw consent.
- **Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS):** On iOS we ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- If you decline personalisation you can still play; you may see less relevant ads.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for advertising-related processing; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) to keep the game working and secure.

## Sharing and transfers
Data is shared only with Google and its advertising partners as described above. Some of these recipients are outside the EEA; Google relies on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Retention
Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Ad-related data is kept by Google under its own policies.

## Your rights
You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Because we do not hold an account about you, requests about advertising data are usually handled by Google; you can also contact us at bennetmarcus19@gmail.com.
